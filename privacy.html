3 · Server Log Files

GitHub, as hosting provider, automatically collects and stores information in server log files that your browser transmits automatically. This may include:

  • IP address of the requesting device
  • Date and time of access
  • Name and URL of the accessed file
  • Referring website (referrer URL)
  • Browser type and operating system
  • Name of your Internet service provider

This data is not merged with other data sources and is used solely for technical operation and security purposes by GitHub as the hosting provider.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR – legitimate interest in detecting errors and ensuring secure operation

4 · Contact via Email

If you contact us by email, the data you provide (your email address, your name if stated, and the content of your message) will be processed solely for the purpose of handling your request.

This data will not be shared with third parties and will be deleted once your inquiry has been fully resolved, unless statutory retention obligations (e.g., commercial or tax law) require longer storage.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (pre-contractual measures / contract performance) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in responding to inquiries)
Retention: Until fully resolved; for business inquiries up to 10 years (§ 257 HGB, § 147 AO)
